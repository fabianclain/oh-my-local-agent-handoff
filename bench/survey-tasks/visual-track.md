# Task: plan what I film, not only what I say


When I write a video script I am only ever writing the words. What I am actually going to film —
the shot, what is on screen, what I am doing with my hands while I talk — stays in my head, and by
the time I sit down to record it is gone. I end up re-inventing it on the spot, badly.

Give every section of a script somewhere to say what happens on screen alongside what is said, and
put that in front of me while I am reading from the teleprompter, so I can see the words and the
shot at the same time.

I would also like to be able to tell at a glance which sections I have not thought about visually
yet, so I know what is left to plan before I start filming.
